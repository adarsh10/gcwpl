About Us

One of India's Leading Refractory Manufacturers

Founded in 1984 and commencing manufacturing in 1992, Golden Ceramic Works (P) Ltd. has grown to become a trusted supplier of shaped and unshaped refractory products to steel plants and foundries across India and beyond.

  • State-of-the-art automated tunnel kilns for precision manufacturing
  • In-house laboratory testing under experienced engineers
  • Export operations to Far East and Middle East regions
  • 10,000 MT shaped + 5,000 MT unshaped refractory per year
