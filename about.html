Who We Are

A Legacy of Industrial Excellence

Golden Ceramic Works (P) Ltd. is one of the leading suppliers of refractories to the steel casting and foundry industries. Incorporated in 1984 with its foundations laid in 1981 by Late Ram Chandra Prasad, the company began manufacturing operations in 1992.

Starting with an initial capacity of 1,500 MT of Roof Set Bricks for Electric Arc Furnaces and Ladle Refining Furnaces, the company has grown to a current capacity of 15,000 MT per annum. Our products are manufactured under closed surveillance by a team of experienced engineers, with rigorous laboratory testing at every stage.

  • Supplying steel plants and foundries across India
  • Exporting to Far East and Middle East regions
  • Full range of shaped and unshaped refractories
  • Automated tunnel kilns + traditional Down Draft Kilns

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

1981

Foundation Laid

Company founded by Late Ram Chandra Prasad in Chirkunda, Dhanbad, Jharkhand.

1984

Formally Incorporated

Golden Ceramic Works formally registered as a private limited company.

1992

Manufacturing Begins

Production of Roof Set Bricks for Electric Arc Furnaces commences at 1,500 MT annually.

2000s

Capacity Expansion

Significant expansion of kiln infrastructure and product range including High Alumina and Fireclay products.

Present

15,000 MT Capacity
